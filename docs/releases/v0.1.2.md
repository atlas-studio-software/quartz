# Quartz 0.1.2

## A new look
- The tab row, the toolbar and the page now float as islands over your wallpaper, with 12 px of air around them and soft shadows. The glass comes from a picture blurred once, so it costs nothing while you work.
- The address bar sits in the middle of the window, and the tab highlight slides from tab to tab.
- The Home page's clock, search, shortcuts, saved tiles and Markets float as islands too. Markets has compact rows, a chart with its own price scale, and a big chart view.
- Settings: Dim goes from 20% to 90% and starts at 20%; a Dim you set yourself is kept. A new Brightness check switch darkens a panel only where the picture behind it is bright, so text stays readable. The Blur and See-through sliders are gone.
- Motion: tabs sink and lift as you switch, pop-overs grow from where they open, and the window rises in once at start. With reduced motion turned on in Windows, everything only fades.

## Fixes
- Lumen: a click on a service, or its Retry, is no longer lost while the list refreshes. Disney+ no longer bounces you home without saying why.
- Pop-overs (find, downloads, site info, Settings) sit just below the toolbar again.
- Reduced motion: nothing slides or scales, and nothing flickers for a frame.
- Tabs: with many tabs, the strip fits them and then scrolls; the tab you pick is always in view, and the edge buttons page without skipping one.
- A restart brings back every tab, and a big session still starts quickly.
- Links that arrive while Quartz is starting are all kept, in order.
- A window on another virtual desktop is no longer frozen, and one left marked covered after sleep redraws by itself.
- The update check makes sure a release is still the latest before it installs it.

## For developers
- The DevTools port is off unless you turn it on in Settings > Claude, and it moves to a free port if its own is taken.
- `--close-tabs=<address>` (with `--keep`) closes tabs from the command line.
