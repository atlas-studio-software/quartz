# Quartz 0.1.2

## A new look
- Quartz now floats over your wallpaper. The tab row, the toolbar and the page sit on panels with soft shadows, and the address bar moves to the centre when there's room.
- A highlight slides to the tab you pick.
- The light and dark themes both get the floating look, and the moon button switches between them. In light, the Dim darkens the picture too, and panels lift toward white where their dark text needs it.
- The Home page gets the same look. The clock stands over the picture, System and Apps are separate cards, and the search box names your search engine. Markets has compact rows and a chart with its own price scale.
- Settings: Dim starts lighter and darkens the picture in both themes. A new Brightness check switch has each panel darken (in light, lighten) only where the picture needs it. The Blur and See-through sliders are gone.
- Motion: tabs and pop-overs animate, and the window rises in when Quartz starts. If Windows is set to reduce motion, everything only fades.

## Fixes
- A restart brings back your web tabs. In a big session, the front tab and up to 7 pinned tabs load first, the rest when you open them.
- Links opened while Quartz is starting are all kept, in order.
- With lots of tabs, they fit, then scroll, keeping the selected tab in view.
- Pop-overs sit just below the toolbar again.
- With reduced motion on, nothing slides, scales or flickers.
- A window on another virtual desktop is no longer treated as frozen, and one that stopped drawing after sleep redraws itself.
- Before a copy built from source updates, Quartz checks it's still the latest release.

## For developers
- The DevTools port is off by default; turn it on in Settings > Claude. If its port is taken, Quartz moves to a free one and says so.
- From a second launch, --close-tabs and --keep close tabs by address prefix.
